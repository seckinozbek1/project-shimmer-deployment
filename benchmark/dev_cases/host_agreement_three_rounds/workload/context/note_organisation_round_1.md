## Position

The Organisation thanks the Host State for its early reply and notes the changes with concern on four points.

Unqualified inviolability is the core of any seat agreement, and the Organisation cannot accept entry on a determination by the authorities alone. It is ready to discuss a presumption of consent in case of fire.

A refund scheme may be workable, but a threshold of 5,000 EUR would exclude most of what the Organisation buys. An arbitration clause is indispensable for an organisation that cannot be sued in the courts of the Host State.

A decision on visas within 14 days is the norm the Organisation needs for its technical missions, and it continues to attach importance to the employment of dependants.

It asks for a termination notice of 24 months, and for an annual contribution of 2,600,000 EUR that covers the full running cost of the seat.
