## Position

The Organisation welcomes the acceptance of arbitration and of express consent in Article 4, which it regards as the most important results of this round.

It asks that the text provide that dependants may apply for a work authorisation, as the Host State accepted in principle in round 2. It wishes to see the administrative arrangement on refunds before it agrees to move that procedure.

It is still studying the new paragraphs of Articles 10 and 11 and reserves its position on them.

It accepts a termination notice of 18 months. It asks that the annual contribution be restored to 2,400,000 EUR, since the line for security services has been cut by 100,000 EUR.
