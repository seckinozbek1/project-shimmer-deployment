# Company A offer to Union B and Union B-2 of 19 October 2024

This document restates the numeric terms of the company proposal that Union B and
Union B-2 received on 19 October 2024 and put to a vote on 23 October 2024, where 64 percent of
the members voted to reject it. Figures are taken from the union's own announcement,
"Striking Union Members to Vote on New Contract Proposal from Company A" (example.org,
October 2024); the contract length is as reported by Outlet 4
(23 October 2024). The labels are the corpus author's fixed schema; the figures are the
sources' own.
