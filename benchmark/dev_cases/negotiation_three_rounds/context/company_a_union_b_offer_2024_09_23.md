# Company A offer to Union B and Union B-2 of 23 September 2024

This document restates the numeric terms of the company offer that Company A presented on
23 September 2024 and called its best and final offer, with a vote deadline of 27 September
2024. Figures are taken as reported by Outlet 1 (23 September 2024), Outlet 2
and Outlet 3 (23 September 2024). The labels are the corpus author's fixed schema; the
figures are the sources' own.

CORRECTION (2026-09-08, after source verification). An earlier version of this file said
terms the sources did not quantify are not written here. That overstated what was checked:

of the following three years, summing to the 30% recorded below), and the corpus author did
not find it. Four real figures were therefore omitted, which is why the per-year terms count
as NEW in the 19 October offer in this pair and produce no comparison record. The label
lines are left as the scored run read them; the consequence is recorded in
docs/fix/STEP_R6_REPORT.md.

offer was not put to a vote; the union declined to schedule one.
