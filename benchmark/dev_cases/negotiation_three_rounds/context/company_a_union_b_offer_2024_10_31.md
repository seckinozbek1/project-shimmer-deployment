This document restates the numeric terms of the company proposal announced on 31 October
2024 and ratified by the members on 4 November 2024 with 59 percent in favour. Figures are
taken from the union's own announcements, "Striking Company A workers to vote Monday on new
proposal" (example.org, 1 November 2024) and "Union Workers At Company A Achieve Contract

which members may take as pay, into the 401(k), or split. Nothing was withdrawn between the
two offers: the same one-time money is present in both, restructured. The label lines below
are left exactly as the scored runs read them, so the run reports remain reproducible; the
