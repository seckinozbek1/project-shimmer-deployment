# Bargaining mandate for reviewing each company offer

These rules are the reviewing side's own mandate for the 2024 Company A and Union B
negotiation. They are the operator's framework, written for this corpus. Each rule names
the term of the offer it is about. Where the mandate states a band, it is the band the
reviewing side set out to reach; the union's public position sought a general wage
increase of 40 percent.

## CONV-N01 general wage increase total

The general wage increase total must sit inside 40% to 45%.

## CONV-N02 year one increase

The year one increase must sit inside 15% to 20%.

## CONV-N03 ratification bonus

The ratification bonus must sit inside $10,000 to $15,000.

## CONV-N04 retirement contributions stated

The company match rate, the company match ceiling and the special company retirement
contribution must be stated in every offer.

## CONV-N05 one-time contribution

Any one-time 401(k) contribution must be stated with its amount, and a one-time 401(k)
contribution withdrawn since the earlier offer must be noted.

## CONV-N06 pension multiplier

The pension multiplier must sit inside $105 to $150 per year of service.

## CONV-N07 incentive minimum

The GPP minimum annual payout must sit inside 4% to 6%.

## CONV-N08 contract length

The contract length must be stated in every offer.
