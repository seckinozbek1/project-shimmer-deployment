# Training data of the judge (T, D-S42 item 2)

Development, exposed. Written 2026-11-06, revised 2026-11-07 after the lead's decisions and the
operator's licence ruling. The sets below are public training splits of related tasks.
ContraDoc, SciTab, ContractNLI, CUAD and FinDVer are not training data in any split. Licences are
stated as read on each set's own card, licence file or README on 2026-11-06; this is a reading,
not legal advice. Nothing here is a benchmark result.

**Overlap with test splits is not checked, by rule (no test material is opened). VitaminC is a
Wikipedia set and ContraDoc draws on Wikipedia, so a test document may share a page with a training
row. Every report on T says so.**

The target format is C's: `benchmark/baselines/choice_judge_prompt.json` on lane/choice, forms
`pair` (Yes contradicts, No does not, Unclear cannot tell) and `claim` (Yes holds, No not met,
Unclear cannot be settled). Yes means opposite classes in the two forms; the label maps are kept
apart. The builder pins the forms (`f4271698...`) and the selector (`doc_judge.select_cells`,
`scripts/cell_selection.py` and `config/cell_selection.json`, `8cc2dcf0...`) and refuses to build
on others.

## The publication rule (operator, 2026-11-07)

"The adapter we publish is trained only on the permissive-licence mix; the share-alike mix is for
development measurement only. If the permissive mix scores clearly worse, tell me before publishing
anything." This is a PUBLICATION rule, not a measurement rule: both mixes are trained and measured.
The `dev_mix` adapter is never published, packaged or copied outside `external/`. Its training file,
`config.json`, `result.json` and the `PUBLICATION.json` beside each adapter say so, and a
share-alike row in the publishable mix refuses the file. Whether an adapter is a derivative work of
its data is the operator's question and is not decided here.

## How the rows are made

`tools/build_judge_training.py`, seed 7, every choice an order of SHA-256 digests, so a rebuild of the
same inputs is byte-identical (proved on fixtures; the real build was made once). The held-out split
is 5 percent by GROUP (premise, page, table) within a FAMILY, so a derived set (same page other case,
TabFact rows removed) splits with its source and no group is on both sides. Rows over 2,048 tokens
are dropped and counted (0 in the final build).

- **Pairs, both orders.** NLI contradiction is Yes; entailment and neutral are No, since C's question
  says passages about different things do not contradict. VitaminC refutes is Yes, supports is No,
  not enough info is Unclear. Same page other case: two evidence sentences of one page from
  different cases, No, judged by nobody; a few may truly conflict.
- **Padding, a declared augmentation.** 30 percent of pairs, chosen by digest with no reference to
  the label, have each passage set inside 30 to 600 words (log-uniform) of sentences from other rows
  of their own set. Then `stratify` keeps, in every cell of (padded or not, length band), the same
  share of each answer, and drops any cell too small to hold all of them. The share per cell is in
  the manifest and below, so length and padding carry no signal for the label. The padding is off
  topic, which a real document is not.
- **Claims.** A TabFact statement on the cells C's selector chooses: Yes holds, No refuted. Unclear:
  the rows the claim names (by the selector's own ranking) are taken out of the table, and the claim
  is asked of what is left. Synthetic: what remains may still settle the claim, and nobody checked.
  The selector refused 7,752 statements (5,089 ties, 2,663 no match); those are not rows.
- **Text shape.** TabFact's text is lower case and tokenized, and VitaminC's has spaced punctuation.
  Neither matches a clean document; held-out loss cannot see this.

## Does QLoRA on Phi-4 fit a 24 GB A10

Yes, at sequences up to 2,048 tokens, microbatch 1. Reasoned, not yet measured on training.

Phi-4 at the pinned revision (config read): `LlamaForCausalLM`, 40 layers, hidden 5,120, MLP
17,920, 40 heads, 10 KV heads, vocabulary 100,352, untied head, the head skipped by quantization.

| Item, at 2,048 tokens | GB |
|---|---|
| Checkpoint on disk, 4-bit layers with bf16 embedding and head | 9.1 |
| `prepare_model_for_kbit_training` makes embedding and head float32 | 2.1 |
| Rank-8 LoRA, seven projections, 32.8 M: float32 weights, gradients, two Adam moments | 0.5 |
| Checkpointed layer inputs, 40 x 2,048 x 5,120, float32 at worst | 1.7 |
| One layer recomputed: eager attention 40 x 2,048 squared x 4 bytes x 3 copies | 2.0 |
| The same layer's MLP and the dequantized weight buffers | 0.8 |
| Logits at the answer position only (one target token) | under 0.1 |
| Total allocated | about 16.2 |

Anchors: J1 peaked at 11.9 GB with this model in inference (D-277); the Qwen2.5-7B QLoRA ran at
9.42 allocated, 11.61 reserved GiB at 992 tokens on the A10 (sampled lower bounds). At that ratio
16.2 GB reserves about 20 GB of the A10's 22 GiB. The longest final row is 1,534 tokens. 4,096
tokens would not fit under eager attention. A 14B model does not fit the operator's 8 GB laptop
card: its weights alone are 9.1 GB.

## The launch: one session, two recipes

One B1 replay bundle, as J1's: `--arm B1`, `--replay-prompts` the session file, one pass
`{"name": "train", "replay": true, "args": ["--paths", "sdpa"]}`. No bound controller file changed;
`benchmark/baselines/` is in `ARCHIVE_EXTRAS`. A batch of two B1 bundles is not admitted (batches
are paired or B3-only), so the session file holds both recipes: `dev_mix` trains and is asked under
`evidence/b1/train/dev_mix/`, then `permissive_mix`, whose last pass writes where the runner counts.

The recipe (`benchmark/baselines/judge_train.py`, the Producer's): 4-bit base through
`prepare_model_for_kbit_training` with gradient checkpointing, LoRA rank 8, alpha 16, dropout .05 on
the seven projections, AdamW 1e-4, linear decay after 20 warmup updates, 16 rows an update,
microbatch 1, eager attention, seed 7, `final_models.verify_runtime` (deterministic algorithms, no
TF32, the pinned `CUBLAS_WORKSPACE_CONFIG`). The target is one token, the answer's, read after the
prompt by `choice_decision.bind`; the loss is its cross entropy over the whole vocabulary, the head
run at that position alone. Checkpoints, fixed in the file: `trained_half` at .5 and `trained_full`
at 1. Question passes: `trained_half`, `trained_full` per recipe; base is not asked, since C asks it
on the same prompts file first. `max_train_seconds` 19,800 per recipe: the run measures its rate
after 20 updates and cuts its schedule only if 716 updates would not fit, which holds both recipes
to the same updates at either rate below. Training is eager and the decision is sdpa, the
Producer's split; the difference is unmeasured.

Digests of the dry assembly on the real questions (`external/s05/P2A/choice_prompts.json`,
`707912dc...`, 4,893 prompts, `prompt_id` and `user_sha256` kept), made with lane/choice's
`doc_judge` imported first: dev_mix recipe `25f0c9b3...`, permissive_mix recipe `a3ad286d...`, one
recipes digest `3d09b0e0...`, the session file 43,893,601 bytes. Rerun after the merge; the
digests hold if nothing they cover changed.

## The judge's success rule (D-S72, D-324, corrected by D-330), written before attempt 2e

Written 8 November 2026, before attempt 2e is sealed and before any reply of it exists, and corrected
the same evening after success-check's attack (D-330). It binds every training attempt of the judge
from 2e on. Changing it is a new decision written before the attempt it governs, never after a read.
Its code is `benchmark/baselines/judge_success.py` (proofs `tools/judge_success_checks.py`).

**What is compared.** The trained judge (the attempt's last checkpoint) against the untrained form:
base Phi-4 (`unsloth/phi-4-bnb-4bit`, revision `098ea917`) on the same choice form. **Both are asked in
the attempt's own session**: the base as the pass `base` on half A, before training. Both take their
threshold on half A by ONE rule (`judge_success.tuning_thresholds`: `choice_read.choose_threshold`,
C_BAR's own, on rows at temperature 1 for both), fixed in code before the seal. The calibration's
temperature is the product's, for the fresh read, and is not used here. C base's earlier decisions on
the same prompts are compared with the session's base, by `prompt_id` and `user_sha256`, and the count
of identical decisions is reported. Read through `choice_read`'s views, labels only through
`tools/score_corpus.py`.

**The tuning material.** `external/s05/P2A/choice_prompts_r1.json` (sha256
`9d5b3e1f35fa1f73747405ea6131acb94430753dd764901e398943b37b382290`), half A by `choice_read.halves`
(salt `20261106:`): 30 ContraDoc and 15 SciTab documents.

**No held-out part exists for the judge.** Counted by ids only, with no key opened (D-330 item 1):
- **SciTab:** all 95 development claims on 29 tables were read by G1, so none is untouched.
- **ContraDoc:** 184 development documents.
  - 65 are named by recorded sets (G1, L11, the dry runs, the labels).
  - 62 are the fresh set's.
  - 5 share a near-duplicate group with a fresh document.
  - 52 share one with a read document.
  - Every one of the 184 texts was also read by T's overlap filter, which dropped training rows on it.
- **Half B is spent.** It was read for T (D-294), and its figures shaped 2e's recipe (L-3), so it is no
  held-out part.
- The fresh 100 and their 9 siblings are excluded.

So **the judge's success is shown on its tuning half only, labelled IN-SAMPLE, and its confirmation is
the system's fresh read** (D-S70, read once by the whole system). `judge_success.require_opening` and
`heldout_sign_test` stand ready should a reserve ever exist. Scoring one is refused unless
`docs/loop/HELDOUT_OPENINGS.md` as committed has the attempt's row and a committed record shows its
tuning pass. The test is one-sided, the sign test on SciTab (named in advance), at alpha 0.05 / 2^k.

**Measures (C_BAR's own, no new rule).** SciTab: macro-F1 of the thresholded view. ContraDoc: net, the
right flags less the false flags.

**Margins.** SciTab +0.05 macro-F1; ContraDoc net +2.

**In-sample success.** On half A:
- the trained judge beats the untrained by its full margin on SciTab, the benchmark named in advance;
- and on ContraDoc it is not worse than the untrained by more than its margin.

It is reported as in-sample, never as a held-out success. D-S72's stopping rule (a gain that holds on
held-out material) cannot be met by the judge alone. **Its stop is the system's fresh read**, which the
lead reads once when every role is ready (D-S70).

**Real progress, per attempt** (D-S72 item 3). On half A, an attempt beats the best earlier attempt (the
untrained form before the first) by at least half a margin on SciTab (+0.025), with ContraDoc not
falling by more than half its margin. An attempt that misses it is pushed to the operator in
FOR_SECKIN, with what was tried, why it did not work from the records, and what the next attempt
changes. The loop then goes on by itself.

**Beside every result** (D-330 item 5, D-S73; `judge_success.BESIDE`):
- B1 (J1, D-277): SciTab .838 macro-F1 on 93 development claims; ContraDoc .567 accuracy on 60
  development documents.
- The D-S73 bars, being checked by table-check:
  - SciTab 65 macro-F1 on three classes (MACE, a 2026 preprint: an 8B planner and executor with a 20B
    verifier, on the whole set);
  - ContraDoc 0.681 document-level accuracy (LLaMA3.2-90B, one judge-and-quote prompt on all 891
    documents, Tan et al. 2026, not an agentic system).
- Shimmer scores development subsets of 15 and 30 documents. The settings differ, so no like-for-like
  claim is made.

**Power, said plainly (L-4).** Half A is about 46 SciTab claims and 30 ContraDoc documents, and in T
only 5 of 13 ContraDoc gold contradictions were reachable. A SciTab margin of .05 is about two to three
claims. No significance is claimed: an in-sample success is a development signal, with its counts and a
sign test beside it. A failure at this size is "not shown at this size", never "training does not help".

## Attempt 2e's recipe, traced to its lessons

- **L-3 (T attempt 1, D-292 to D-294).** Held-out accuracy flat after the midpoint, and benchmarks
  barely moved: the pair rows were not in the run-time question's form. So 2e builds its pairs from
  the run-time proposer's own pairs on web documents, with figure-changed twins, and its claims in
  the run-time form with its tools block. The benchmarks' source families stay under a quarter
  (21.1 percent). There is no pair Unclear and no padding, with Yes at 20 percent of pairs.
- **L-4 (the D-S45 either attempt, D-297, D-316).** An effect chosen in sample misled. So 2e's
  success rule above states its in-sample label, names SciTab in advance, fixes both judges' thresholds
  by one rule in code before the seal, asks the base in the same session, and states power beside
  every figure. Half B is spent (D-330).
- **Unchanged from T, so the comparison isolates the data:** Phi-4 14B 4-bit, rank 8, alpha 16,
  dropout .05, AdamW 1e-4 linear with 20 warmup updates, 16 rows an update, eager attention, seed 7,
  strict determinism. One pass over 710 updates. Checkpoints .5 and 1. Permissive mix only.
