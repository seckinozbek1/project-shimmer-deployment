## Items

### S4a. Selection (research done by three read-only subagents; results in the private research files)
1. At least ten candidates researched from their own cards, licence files and papers: ContractNLI, CUAD, MAUD, the LegalBench ContractNLI tasks, the LegalBench rule-application tasks, ContraDoc, LLM-AggreFact, SummEdits, AIReg-Bench, DocNLI, TabFact, SciTab, FEVEROUS, FinQA, TAT-QA, FinDVer (16). For each: what it is, size, document type and length, decisions per document, labels and evidence spans, splits, official metric, licence, access, evaluation use, contamination, fit, source address, version or commit, checksums.
2. Choice: build an adapter for every candidate open to download without H13 issues and fitting well; aim five or more, at least two document domains (P20). Proposed: ContraDoc (news, stories, Wikipedia; Apache-2.0; internal contradiction, clean documents measure false alarms), CUAD (commercial contracts; CC BY 4.0; presence of required clauses; published md5), SciTab (scientific tables; MIT; supports, refutes, not enough info), FinDVer (financial filings; MIT; claims needing arithmetic over long filings), SummEdits (summaries against sources, ten domains; Apache-2.0; upstream source licences to be checked by B-dev before use, and any NC or ND source excluded). Five adapters, at least four domains.
3. Rejected or deferred with reasons: ContractNLI (best fit, but a click-through acceptance: H13, to `FOR_SECKIN.md` with the exact terms as a candidate for a later run); LLM-AggreFact (gated access and CC BY-ND: H13 and an ND licence, to `FOR_SECKIN.md`); MAUD (pre-extracted passages, no whole document); the LegalBench ContractNLI tasks (drawn from the ContractNLI test set, binary, no spans); the LegalBench rule-application tasks (short fact patterns, hand-graded application); AIReg-Bench (licence conflict between its card and its repository, small and synthetic); DocNLI (could not be verified); TabFact and FEVEROUS (no document under review, open-domain or single short tables); FinQA and TAT-QA (free-text or numeric answers with no clean mapping to verdicts).
4. `docs/benchmarks/SELECTION.md` records each chosen benchmark's source address, version or commit, and published checksums (B-dev's input), the rejected candidates with reasons, the gated ones, and later the dry-run results labelled development. The preregistration ranks benchmarks by fit, then by measured cost per scored decision (S5); S8 seals at least three in that order; fewer than three qualifying would be recorded and met together with B's synthetic tests (not a block).
