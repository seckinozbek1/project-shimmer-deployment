# Alpha Holdings Merger Review

Decision of the Competition Authority of Synthetica, No. 12 of 2021.

## I. Procedural history

On 14 March 2021 the Authority received a notification concerning the proposed
acquisition of Beacon Logistics by Alpha Holdings. The parties submitted the
notification under the merger-control regime in force at the time.

## II. The relevant market

The Authority defined the relevant product market as the supply of regional
cold-chain freight services. The geographic market was held to be national in
scope, for the reasons set out in the following paragraphs.

## III. Assessment

The concentration did not give rise to a significant impediment to effective
competition. Market shares remained moderate and entry barriers were found to be
low. Accordingly, the Authority cleared the transaction without conditions.
